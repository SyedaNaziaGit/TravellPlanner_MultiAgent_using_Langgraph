## 🗓️ Itinerary
**Trip Overview:**
Get ready for a 5-day adventure in Goa, one of India's most vibrant and beautiful destinations! This trip is perfect for travelers looking for a mix of relaxation, culture, and fun. Since you're traveling from Bangalore, we'll explore affordable options to make the most of your trip under INR 10,000.

**Day-by-Day Itinerary:**

**Day 1: Arrival and Acclimatization**

* Morning: Take an overnight bus from Bangalore to Goa (approximately 14-15 hours, INR 1,500 - 2,000 return)
* Afternoon: Check-in to a budget-friendly hostel in Panaji or nearby areas (INR 800 - 1,200 per night)
* Evening: Explore the local market, visit the Immaculate Conception Church, and enjoy the sunset at Miramar Beach

**Day 2: North Goa Adventure**

* Morning: Visit the famous Calangute Beach, try water sports, and enjoy a hearty breakfast at a local café (INR 200 - 300 per person)
* Afternoon: Head to Baga Beach, explore the nearby shops and eateries, and take a dip in the Arabian Sea
* Evening: Enjoy the vibrant nightlife at Tito's Lane or Curlies Beach Shack

**Day 3: South Goa Exploration**

* Morning: Visit the stunning Palolem Beach, take a boat ride, and enjoy a relaxing morning (INR 500 - 800 per person)
* Afternoon: Explore the historic town of Margao, visit the Margao Market, and try some local cuisine
* Evening: Enjoy a peaceful evening at the Colva Beach, watch the sunset, and try some seafood

**Day 4: Cultural Experience**

* Morning: Visit the ancient Basilica of Bom Jesus, explore the Se Cathedral, and learn about Goa's rich history
* Afternoon: Take a stroll along the Mandovi River, visit the Reis Magos Fort, and enjoy the scenic views
* Evening: Enjoy a traditional Goan dinner at a local restaurant (INR 500 - 800 per person)

**Day 5: Departure**

* Morning: Spend some time shopping for souvenirs, exploring the local market, or relaxing at the beach
* Afternoon: Take the bus back to Bangalore (approximately 14-15 hours, INR 1,500 - 2,000 return)

**Recommendations:**

* **Places to Eat:**
	+ Infanteria for Goan cuisine
	+ The Fish Factory for seafood
	+ Lila Café for breakfast and snacks
* **Places to Stay:**
	+ Hostel La Vie En Rose (INR 800 - 1,200 per night)
	+ The Funky Monkey Hostel (INR 600 - 1,000 per night)
	+ Budget hotels like Hotel Palacio De Goa (INR 1,500 - 2,500 per night)
* **Tips:**
	+ Book your bus tickets and accommodation in advance to avoid high prices
	+ Try local transportation like buses or autos to save money
	+ Bargain at local markets and shops to get the best deals
	+ Be respectful of the local culture and environment

**Budget Breakdown:**

* Transportation: INR 3,000 - 4,000 (bus tickets)
* Accommodation: INR 2,000 - 3,000 (hostel or budget hotel)
* Food and activities: INR 3,000 - 4,000 (average INR 600 - 800 per day)
* Total: INR 8,000 - 10,000

This itinerary should give you a great balance of relaxation, culture, and adventure in Goa, all within your budget of INR 10,000. Enjoy your trip!
