## 🗓️ Itinerary
**Trip Overview:**
Get ready to experience the vibrant culture, stunning beaches, and lively nightlife of Goa with this 3-day itinerary, all within your budget of 15k! This trip is perfect for a group of friends looking to unwind and have a blast together. With a mix of popular sights, unique experiences, and practical tips, you'll be sure to make the most of your time in Goa.

**Day-by-Day Itinerary:**

**Day 1: North Goa Adventure**

* Morning: Arrive in Goa and check-in to your hotel. Consider staying at **The Zuri White Sands Goa Resort & Casino** or **Taj Fort Aguada Resort & Spa, Goa** for a luxurious experience. (9:00 am - 10:00 am)
* Afternoon: Head to **Baga Beach** and enjoy water sports like parasailing, jet-skiing, or simply relax on the beach. (10:30 am - 1:30 pm)
* Evening: Experience the Goa nightlife at **Titos** or **Mambos**, two of the most popular beach clubs in North Goa. (7:00 pm - 11:00 pm)

**Day 2: Explore Iconic Spots**

* Morning: Visit **Fort Aguada**, a 17th-century Portuguese fort that offers breathtaking views of the Arabian Sea. (9:00 am - 10:30 am)
* Afternoon: Explore **Chapora Fort**, another historic fort that's perfect for a scenic hike and stunning views. (11:00 am - 1:00 pm)
* Evening: Enjoy a sunset dinner at **Sublime**, a beachfront restaurant in Morjim, known for its delicious seafood and romantic ambiance. (6:00 pm - 9:00 pm)

**Day 3: South Goa Delights**

* Morning: Visit **Colva Beach**, a picturesque beach in South Goa that's perfect for swimming and relaxing. (9:00 am - 12:00 pm)
* Afternoon: Explore the **Old Goa** area, including the **Se Cathedral** and **Basilica of Bom Jesus**, two of the most iconic landmarks in Goa. (1:00 pm - 3:30 pm)
* Evening: Enjoy a farewell dinner at **Infantaria**, a popular restaurant in Panjim, known for its traditional Goan cuisine. (7:00 pm - 10:00 pm)
