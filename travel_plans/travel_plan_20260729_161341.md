## 🗓️ Itinerary
**Trip Overview:**
Welcome to the City of Light! Your 5-day Paris trip is going to be an unforgettable experience. Paris, the capital of France, is a haven for art, fashion, cuisine, and romance. Get ready to immerse yourself in the city's stunning architecture, world-class museums, and charming streets. This itinerary is designed to provide a perfect blend of popular sights and off-the-beaten-path experiences, ensuring that you make the most of your time in Paris.

**Day-by-Day Itinerary:**

**Day 1: Arrival and Exploring Montmartre**

* Morning: Arrive in Paris and check-in to your hotel. We recommend staying in the Latin Quarter or Le Marais for easy access to main attractions.
* Afternoon: Visit the iconic Sacré-Cœur Basilica in Montmartre, explore the charming streets, and enjoy the bohemian vibe.
* Evening: Enjoy a traditional French dinner at a bistro in Montmartre, such as Le Comptoir du Relais or Chez L'Ami Jean.

**Day 2: Parisian Landmarks**

* Morning: Start the day with a visit to the iconic Eiffel Tower ( Ticket price: around $17). You can take the stairs or elevator to the top for breathtaking views.
* Afternoon: Take a scenic river cruise along the Seine (around $15) to admire the city's landmarks and bridges.
* Evening: Enjoy a cabaret show at the world-famous Moulin Rouge (tickets start at around $100).

**Day 3: Museums and Gardens**

* Morning: Visit the Louvre Museum (around $18), home to the Mona Lisa and an impressive collection of art and artifacts.
* Afternoon: Stroll through the beautiful Jardin du Luxembourg, a tranquil oasis in the heart of the city.
* Evening: Enjoy a wine and cheese tasting experience at a local vineyard or wine bar, such as La Grande Epicerie de Paris.

**Day 4: Royal Paris**

* Morning: Visit the Palace of Versailles (around $20), a former royal residence with stunning gardens and fountain shows.
* Afternoon: Explore the trendy Marais neighborhood, known for its fashionable boutiques, art galleries, and hip restaurants.
* Evening: Enjoy a dinner at a Michelin-starred restaurant, such as Le Grand Vefour or Septime.

**Day 5: Markets and Montparnasse**

* Morning: Visit the historic Marché aux Puces de Saint-Ouen (flea market) for a unique shopping experience.
* Afternoon: Explore the charming neighborhood of Montparnasse, known for its artistic heritage, cafes, and street performers.
* Evening: Enjoy a farewell dinner at a classic Parisian bistro, such as Le Dome or La Closerie des Lilas.

**Recommendations:**

* **Places to Eat:**
	+ Le Comptoir du Relais (Montmartre)
	+ Chez L'Ami Jean (Montmartre)
	+ Le Grand Vefour (Michelin-starred)
	+ Septime (Michelin-starred)
	+ La Grande Epicerie de Paris (wine and cheese)
* **Places to Stay:**
	+ Hôtel Astra Opéra - Astotel (around $150 per night)
	+ Hotel Eiffel Seine (around $120 per night)
	+ Maison Souquet (luxury, around $300 per night)
	+ Le Bristol Paris (luxury, around $500 per night)
* **Tips:**
	+ Buy a Paris Museum Pass for priority entry to many attractions.
	+ Use public transportation or walk/bike whenever possible to avoid traffic.
	+ Don't forget to try a classic French croissant or baguette at a local bakery!

* Food and drink: around $500-$800 (depending on the restaurants and cafes)
